## Independent uncertainty check

Human review independently reconstructed the case-level binary score vectors from the committed per-case/metrics evidence and repeated the paired bootstrap procedure using 20,000 resamples and seed 0. The resulting primary-domain/non-primary deltas and 95% intervals match the committed evidence:

- Math specialist - General, Mathematics: **+29.17 pp**, 95% CI **[+16.67, +41.67] pp**;
- Math specialist - General, Coding: **-37.50 pp**, 95% CI **[-52.08, -22.92] pp**;
- Coder specialist - General, Coding: **-10.42 pp**, 95% CI **[-22.92, +2.08] pp**;
- Coder specialist - General, Mathematics: **+12.50 pp**, 95% CI **[-2.08, +27.08] pp**.

These figures are accepted as evidence for A6; this review does not assign the final Gate result.

## Non-blocking metadata issue

`inference-receipt.json` labels a field `load_elapsed_seconds`, but the runner starts that timer before model loading and reads it only after all 96 generations finish. The value therefore includes model loading plus generation and must not be interpreted as model-load latency. It does not affect prompts, outputs, scoring, or comparability and is not a blocker for A6. Future execution tooling should rename or measure this field correctly.
